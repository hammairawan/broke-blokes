Stay Un-Broke

Small changes that keep money in your pocket. Pick a topic, then use the bill splitter when you eat out with friends.

Track every expense for two weeks

Write down each purchase. Most people find a few small, regular costs that add up to more than they expected.

Pay yourself first

Move a fixed amount to savings on the day money arrives, then budget with what is left.

Use a waiting rule

For any unplanned purchase, wait 24 hours. If you still want it and it fits your budget, buy it.

Audit subscriptions monthly

List every recurring charge. Cancel what you did not use this month, and share plans only where the provider allows it.

Cook in batches

Cooking larger portions and storing them costs less per meal than buying meals out or ordering in.

Plan a weekly shopping list

Shop with a list and a set amount. Planning meals reduces food waste and impulse purchases.

Carry water and a snack

Buying drinks and snacks between classes is a common hidden cost. A refillable bottle removes it.

Share rides and use passes

Carpool with classmates where it is safe and legal, and check whether student passes cut your fares.

Walk or cycle short trips

Short journeys by foot or bike cost nothing and are often as fast as waiting for transport.

Borrow or buy used textbooks

Check the library, senior students and second-hand listings before buying new. Confirm the edition first.

Ask about student discounts

Software, devices and services often have education pricing. Keep your student card or email ready.

Group bill splitter

Split a meal or trip fairly. Choose an equal split, or split by what each person ordered.

Names are optional. In "by what each person ordered" mode, enter each person's items. Anything left over on the bill is shared equally.

Who pays what

Amounts use whatever currency you entered.
